Anything But Shadows

itch.io page

To my memory, this was the first project I really finished. I believe it was for a game jam? Or maybe it started off in one. I can't quite remember; it was a long time ago. But, looking back on it, it's impressive that I was able to do everything on my own-- the animations, sound, models, code, etc. At this point I had only been working on games for a year or two.

I also remember being so passionate about the project. The vision for it my mind was so enticing and I had so many ideas for it. If I remember right, it was meant to be inside of a computer or virtual world. It would be the first of three games which slowly reveal that fact. Premature trilogy planning has always been a signature aspect of my idea phase.
